Privacy policy: Fork It Open publisher

Terms: terms of service

Last updated: 8 November 2026

"Fork It Open publisher" is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Fork It Open to manage that channel. It has no other users.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data the tool accesses

  • With the owner's Google OAuth consent: permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, add subtitles and manage the metadata of the owner's own channel (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl).
  • Read-only statistics of the owner's own channel and videos, such as views and watch time (scope yt-analytics.readonly).

The tool does not access data of any other user or channel.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Uploads and metadata are sent only to YouTube.
  • Channel statistics are used to produce a private daily summary for the channel owner, delivered to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computers. Statistics are kept for at most 30 days and then deleted or refreshed.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool uses no cookies.

Deleting data

Stored statistics are deleted automatically after 30 days. All locally stored data and tokens can be deleted on request by writing to the contact address below; they are then deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (third-party access); the local tokens are then deleted.
